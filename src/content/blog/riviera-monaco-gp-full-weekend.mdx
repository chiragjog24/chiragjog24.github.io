## 05 — Three critical mitigations

Two consecutive 12-hour Monaco days are the most demanding stretch of the entire trip. If any of these three slip, the GP weekend turns from once-in-a-lifetime into fibromyalgia flare. Treat them as non-negotiable.

### 01 · Friday rest

The day before is the day that matters. Friday 5 June is a full hotel-pool-and-balcony rest day. No outings. No "quick lunch in Èze." No "we feel fine, let's pop into Old Nice." The single biggest determinant of whether Saturday and Sunday are bearable is how rested you are when Saturday morning arrives. Treat it as scheduled medical recovery.

### 02 · Sunday taxi

Pre-book a private car for the post-race departure. €200 for the family is the single best fibromyalgia investment in this entire trip. The Monaco train station post-race on Sunday evening is the worst physical environment of the 12 days — 50,000+ people queuing for trains, multiple platforms, standing-only trains, 30–45 minute waits. A pre-booked taxi via Hyatt concierge (book 4 weeks before) gets you door-to-door in 35 minutes vs 90+ minutes by train. Do not try to save money here.

### 03 · Ear defenders

Real foam earplugs from India. Don't trust on-site. F1 cars at the Monaco tunnel exit hit ~110 dB. Café de Paris Massenet seats are loud — exhilarating for a 14-year-old, exhausting for fibromyalgia ears across 6+ hours, two days running. Bring proper foam earplugs (Loop Engage, Eargasm, or Mack's foam) and child-rated ear defenders. On-site sellers price-gouge during GP weekend. Pack three sets per person — keep one set at the hotel as backup, two for venue use.

## 06 — Stay

### Versailles · 2 nights (31 May – 2 Jun) — Trianon Palace Versailles · Waldorf Astoria

5* hotel literally on the grounds of the Versailles palace estate. Indoor pool, full Guerlain spa, gardens, vegetarian-aware dining. Real fibromyalgia tools (spa, pool, gardens to walk slowly through) before the demanding Riviera-and-GP week ahead.

- 30–40 min RER C + Métro to Gare de Lyon for the TGV south
- Direct access to Versailles château gardens (free)
- Indoor pool + spa = real recovery tools
- Alternative: Pavillon Henri IV (Saint-Germain-en-Laye) or Auberge du Jeu de Paume (Chantilly)

### Nice · 8 nights (2–9 Jun) · GP-week premium — Hyatt Regency Palais de la Méditerranée

Sea View Junior Suite on the Promenade des Anglais. Year-round indoor-outdoor pool, sauna, steam, hammam. World of Hyatt benefits.

- Walkable to Old Nice (~10 min), Gare de Nice-Ville (~15 min)
- 24-hour room service for dietary control
- GP-week pricing at peak — book early on refundable rate

### Paris dipping rules — if you venture into central Paris on Day 2

> **Five rules to skip the tourist traps:**
>
> 1. **Don't eat within 300m** of the Eiffel Tower, Louvre, Notre-Dame, Champs-Élysées, or Sacré-Cœur. Guaranteed tourist-trap zone.
> 2. **Skip restaurants with laminated photo menus** or staff trying to wave you in. Universal fast-food-tourist signal.
> 3. **For lunch, head to Le Marais** (3rd/4th arr.) instead — vegetarian-friendly bistros, walkable, 15–20 min Métro from the Eiffel Tower.
> 4. **Skip the Tuileries tuk-tuk rides.** Skip **Angelina** (the famous hot chocolate place — long lines, overrated).
> 5. **Use [Le Fooding](https://lefooding.com)** for credible restaurant picks. Skip TripAdvisor and TikTok recommendations.

## 09 — Action checklist

In priority order. Items are sequenced so each one unblocks the next.

1. **Send Senate Grand Prix inquiry email — today.** Six-question inquiry covering: Saturday + Sunday Café de Paris 1st Floor pricing for 2 adults + 1 junior (age 14, junior rate?), vegetarian 3-course menu both days, non-alcoholic open bar substitution for the 14-year-old, designated table allocation, cancellation policy, accessibility on 1st Floor (stairs / lift). Also request quote on Nice ↔ Monaco coach transfer add-on for Saturday. Senate's UK number: +44 1342 830 490.

2. **Lock Hyatt Regency Palais de la Méditerranée — this week.** Sea View Junior Suite, 2–9 June 2026. Refundable rate. World of Hyatt account if you have one. GP-week pricing at peak.

3. **Pay Senate deposit once vegetarian + junior pricing confirmed — within 7 days.** Typically 30–50% deposit at booking. Balance due closer to event. Get cancellation policy in writing first. €12,000 total for the family of 3 (€4,000pp × 3).

4. **Hold Trianon Palace Versailles — this week.** 2 nights, 31 May – 2 Jun. Junior Suite, refundable rate. Confirm vegetarian dining capability and indoor pool / spa access. Book pre-arranged transfer from CDG (~45 min).

5. **Book Mumbai ↔ Paris flights.** Mumbai → Paris CDG arriving 31 May. Paris CDG → Mumbai departing 9–10 Jun. Direct AF / AI preferred.

6. **Book TGV INOUI Paris → Nice for 2 Jun.** 1st class, upper deck, table-of-4 (carré), right side facing forward. SNCF Connect.

7. **Pre-book private car Monaco → Nice for Sun 7 Jun ~18:30 — 1 month before.** Via Hyatt concierge or Uber Black. Confirm GP-day surge pricing in writing.

8. **Travel insurance with pre-existing condition cover — 1 week.** Tata AIG / ICICI Lombard / Bajaj Allianz "Travel Prime" or AXA Schengen.

9. **VFS Mumbai Schengen appointment — 2 weeks.** Documentation pack: cover letter, ITRs, bank statements, employment letter, school letter, all bookings + Senate confirmation, insurance.

10. **Order foam earplugs and ear defenders before travel.** Loop Engage / Eargasm for adults, child-rated noise-reducing earmuffs for the 14-year-old. 3 sets per person — two-day exposure makes redundancy worth it.

11. **Brief both Hyatt concierges on dietary needs and accessibility.** Email Versailles and Nice 2 weeks pre-arrival. Vegetarian (no meat / fish / seafood, eggs and dairy fine). Sat + Sun late-evening room service confirmed.

12. **Confirm Senate ticket / wristband collection logistics.** Usually from Hôtel Hermitage Friday morning or Saturday morning. Get exact location, time, ID requirements in writing. For 2-day package you collect both days' wristbands at once.

13. **Book Bellet AOC vineyard visit for Wed 3 Jun morning.** Email Château de Bellet (chateaudebellet.com) or Château de Crémat 2–3 weeks in advance.
