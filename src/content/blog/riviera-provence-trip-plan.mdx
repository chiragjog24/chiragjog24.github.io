## 01 — Don't fight the GP weekend. Leave the coast.

When fifty thousand people descend on Monaco, the entire Côte d'Azur tightens — hotels surge, trains crowd, restaurants fill. The smartest move isn't to hide in Nice; it's to slip away to Provence and come back when it's over.

### The reasoning

**Friday morning** we board the direct TGV at Nice-Ville. Two hours and fifty-six minutes later, we're in Avignon — the medieval papal city, surrounded by Provençal countryside, vineyards, lavender fields, Roman ruins, and the kind of slow-paced rural France that has nothing to do with Formula 1.

**Three nights** in a Relais & Châteaux country hotel just outside Avignon. **Saturday**: Saint-Rémy-de-Provence (Van Gogh's village) and the dramatic fortress town of Les Baux. **Sunday morning**: cellar visit and tasting at a Châteauneuf-du-Pape estate, then the ruined papal château at the village summit overlooking vineyards stretching to Mont Ventoux. **Sunday afternoon** we ride the TGV back to Nice as the GP race finishes. **Monday**: Monaco, the morning after.

### What this trades

Eight Riviera nights become five — but the three you give up were the GP-week stress days anyway. You replace them with countryside calm, gastronomy, lavender season, and a genuine "second France" experience.

A single extra TGV booking in each direction. One additional hotel reservation. Marginally higher total cost (~₹1–2 lakh) for materially better experience. For fibromyalgia, this is the right move: countryside hotel = real rest, not pretending-to-rest amid GP crowds.

## 03 — The Provence Escape

![Lavender fields at sunset in Provence](https://images.pexels.com/photos/32992520/pexels-photo-32992520.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1600)

Avignon is two hours fifty-six minutes from Nice by direct TGV — far enough to feel like another country, close enough to come back without effort. Three nights at a Relais & Châteaux country house, with Saturday in Van Gogh's Saint-Rémy and Sunday morning in the vineyards of Châteauneuf-du-Pape. The trip's secret weapon.

### Base — Auberge de Cassagne & Spa

5* Relais & Châteaux property 8 km from Avignon TGV station. 49 rooms, Provençal stone-and-tile architecture, two restaurants (one Michelin-starred), full spa with hammam and indoor pool, gardens and outdoor pool, vineyard views. Quiet, walkable grounds — ideal for fibromyalgia recovery.

- Hotel arranges TGV station pickup
- Vegetarian menu in advance — they accommodate easily
- Spa treatments bookable on arrival
- Old Avignon 15 min by taxi for an evening visit

### Alternative — La Mirande, Avignon

5* Relais & Châteaux, 27 rooms, in the literal shadow of the Palais des Papes. A meticulously restored 14th-century cardinal's palace. Cooking classes, courtyard garden, exceptional service. Pick this if you prefer urban historical immersion over countryside calm.

### Saturday — Provence Countryside Day (light walking)

![Medieval ruins of Les Baux-de-Provence on the limestone cliffs](https://images.pexels.com/photos/14079665/pexels-photo-14079665.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1600)

Private driver from hotel.

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 10:00 | Leisurely Provençal breakfast at hotel. No rush. |
| 11:00 | Private driver pickup. 30 min drive through olive groves and vineyards to Saint-Rémy-de-Provence. |
| 11:30 | **Brief Saint-Rémy stop** — sit at a café terrace on Place Favier or Place de la République. Watch the Saturday market from a chair if it's running. ~30 min, no walking tour. |
| 12:30 | **Long Provençal lunch** at a vineyard estate restaurant — ratatouille, tian, tomato tart, local rosé. The food is the destination. |
| 14:30 | 15-min drive to **Carrières de Lumières** in the limestone quarries below Les Baux. *Immersive art projection experience inside a cool, dark, fully-accessible quarry.* You sit on benches or stand still while monumental art moves around you on 14m walls. |
| 15:45 | Driver takes you to a **scenic overlook of Les Baux** — see the dramatic hilltop fortress without climbing the cobblestone village. Photos, 15 min, back in the car. |
| 16:30 | Driver returns to hotel. Pool / spa / hammam before dinner. |
| 19:30 | Dinner at hotel. Côtes du Rhône with the meal. Sleep well. |

### Sunday — Châteauneuf-du-Pape Morning

Vineyards · Papal château · TGV home.

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 08:30 | Leisurely breakfast at Auberge de Cassagne. Pack overnight bag. |
| 09:30 | Private driver pickup (~€200, half-day). 25-min drive to Châteauneuf-du-Pape. |
| 10:00 | **Estate visit + tasting at Château La Nerthe** (or Domaine du Vieux Télégraphe). Cellar tour, vineyard walk, guided tasting of 4–5 wines. Pre-booked, in English, vegetarian-aware. ~90 minutes. |
| 11:30 | Drive into **Châteauneuf-du-Pape village**. Walk up to the ruins of the 14th-century papal summer château — sweeping views over vineyards, the Rhône, Mont Ventoux. |

| 14:30 | Hotel checkout. Driver to Avignon TGV station (15 min). |
| 16:30 | **TGV Avignon → Nice** (1st class, direct, ~3h). Reading, Provence countryside fading into Riviera coast. |
| 19:30 | Arrive Nice-Ville. Taxi to Hyatt. Light dinner room service. Sleep early. |

### Châteauneuf-du-Pape — Three estates worth knowing

- **Château La Nerthe** — 18th-century estate, one of the oldest in Châteauneuf, beautiful park and historic cellars. Most "tour-ready" of the prestige estates. English-speaking guides. Vegetarian lunch sometimes available. *Recommended pick.*
- **Domaine du Vieux Télégraphe** — Brunier family, biodynamic, less touristy, more authentic working-domain feel. Great if you prefer agriculture-and-soil over château grandeur.
- **Château de Beaucastel** — among the most prestigious globally, biodynamic since the 1950s. Harder to book, more formal. For serious wine collectors.

All three need **advance booking** (3–4 weeks minimum, longer for prestige estates). Auberge de Cassagne's concierge can arrange. Per-person tasting fee: ~€25–60 depending on estate and wine flight.

### Skip-list — what NOT to add

- **Don't add a second estate visit on Sunday.** One estate + the village is plenty; two becomes a wine slog.
- **Don't add Aix-en-Provence.** Worth its own trip; one Provence base is enough.
- **Don't try to do Avignon proper extensively.** Old town is steep cobblestones; Palais des Papes interior renovation status worth verifying for 2026.
- **Don't book a rental car for Sunday.** A driver costs ~€200 for the half-day and means you can taste freely — driving plus tasting is a non-starter.
- **Don't try to see lavender fields.** Peak bloom is late June through mid-July — too early for your dates.

## 05 — Three cities, three hotels

Paris bookends are short and central. Provence is a country-house Relais & Châteaux for three nights. Nice is a sea-view single base for five nights — vegetarian-aware in-room dining, full spa, walking distance to the train.

### Versailles · 2 nights (31 May – 2 Jun) — Trianon Palace Versailles · Waldorf Astoria

5* hotel literally on the grounds of the Versailles palace estate. Indoor pool, full Guerlain spa, gardens, vegetarian-aware dining. Quiet at evening hours once day-trippers leave the gardens. The 17th-century engineering of the Versailles waterworks is genuinely interesting for any engineer-in-training without requiring a museum visit — you walk through it on hotel grounds.

- 30–40 min RER C + Métro to Gare de Lyon for the TGV south
- Direct walking access to Versailles château gardens (no entry fee for grounds)
- Indoor pool + spa — real fibromyalgia tools, not just amenities
- Alternative: Pavillon Henri IV (Saint-Germain-en-Laye, 5*) or Auberge du Jeu de Paume (Chantilly, Relais & Châteaux)

### Provence · 3 nights (5 – 8 Jun) — Auberge de Cassagne & Spa

5* Relais & Châteaux country house, 8 km from Avignon TGV. Two restaurants (one Michelin-starred), full spa, indoor and outdoor pools, gardens, vineyard views. Quiet, walkable grounds — built for recovery.

- Hotel arranges TGV station transfer
- Vegetarian Provençal menu pre-arranged with kitchen
- Private driver bookable through hotel for Saint-Rémy / Châteauneuf-du-Pape excursions
- Alternative: La Mirande (5*, in central Avignon, 27 rooms, by Palais des Papes)

### Nice · 5 nights (2–5 Jun + 7–10 Jun) — Hyatt Regency Palais de la Méditerranée

![Old Nice — Vieux Nice — historic streets and pastel facades](https://images.pexels.com/photos/29872514/pexels-photo-29872514.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1600)

**Sea View Junior Suite** on the Promenade des Anglais. Year-round indoor-outdoor pool, sauna, steam, hammam — fibromyalgia tools, not just amenities. World of Hyatt benefits.

- Walkable to Old Nice (~10 min), Gare de Nice-Ville (~15 min)
- 24-hour room service for dietary control on flare days
- Request: high floor, sea side, double-glazing confirmed
- **Note:** Book as a single 8-night stay 2–10 Jun; the 3 Provence nights (5–8 Jun) are simply absent. Hotel keeps your room and main luggage; pack overnight bag for Provence.

### Logistics — one Nice booking, leave bags behind

Don't check out and re-check-in at the Hyatt for the Provence trip. Book a continuous 8-night stay and simply leave your main luggage in the room. Pack just an overnight bag for Provence. The hotel will keep the room exactly as you left it.

This avoids: re-check-in queues on a Sunday evening when you arrive tired, room-allocation roulette (you might lose your sea view), and the cognitive overhead of repacking everything twice. Most full-service luxury hotels are happy to do this — confirm in advance with the concierge.

### Paris dipping rules — if you venture into central Paris on Day 2

> **Five rules to skip the tourist traps:**
>
> 1. **Don't eat within 300m** of the Eiffel Tower, Louvre, Notre-Dame, Champs-Élysées, or Sacré-Cœur. Guaranteed tourist-trap zone — overpriced, mediocre, slow service.
> 2. **Skip restaurants with laminated photo menus** or staff standing outside trying to wave you in. Universal fast-food-tourist signal anywhere in Paris.
> 3. **For lunch, head to Le Marais** (3rd/4th arr.) instead — vegetarian-friendly bistros, walkable, 15–20 min Métro from the Eiffel Tower area. The Latin Quarter (away from Rue de la Huchette) and Canal Saint-Martin are also good.
> 4. **Skip the Tuileries tuk-tuk rides.** Skip **Angelina** (the famous hot chocolate place — long lines, overrated). Skip the Mona Lisa scrum at the Louvre if you visit.
> 5. **Use [Le Fooding](https://lefooding.com)** for credible restaurant picks. Skip TripAdvisor rankings and TikTok recommendations — both heavily skewed by tourists, not locals.

## 06 — Built for recovery

Fibromyalgia and a post-infectious gut condition are the silent constraints shaping this entire plan. Every choice — train over flight, single base, sea-view balcony, deliberate rest days — flows from them.

- **No more than one outing per active day.** Mornings only; afternoons reserved for hotel rest.
- **All trains 1st class.** The 2nd-class crowd-and-noise difference is meaningful for fibromyalgia.
- **Dietary control through hotel room service.** Vegetarian, low-FODMAP-aware, pre-arranged with kitchens 2 weeks before arrival.
- **Pre-booked private taxis** at the two highest-stress moments: GP-zone return Sunday evening; possible Saturday-Provence-driver pickup.
- **Spa as medical tool.** Hyatt Nice's hammam, Auberge de Cassagne's full spa — book treatments on arrival for muscle relaxation.

## 09 — Action checklist

In priority order. Items are sequenced so each one unblocks the next.

1. **Lock the Hyatt Regency Palais de la Méditerranée — this week.** Sea View Junior Suite, **continuous 2–10 June 2026 booking** (do NOT split into two stays). Refundable rate to start. Confirm room held with main luggage during 5–8 Jun Provence trip. GP-week pricing at peak.

2. **Hold Auberge de Cassagne & Spa, Provence — this week.** 3 nights, 5–8 Jun 2026. Refundable rate. Email reservations directly. Confirm vegetarian menu capability and TGV station transfer.

3. **Book Mumbai ↔ Paris flights — this week.** Mumbai → Paris CDG arriving 31 May. Paris CDG → Mumbai departing 10 Jun evening. Direct AF / AI preferred for fewer transfers.

4. **Hold Trianon Palace Versailles — this week.** 2 nights, 31 May – 2 Jun. Junior Suite, refundable rate. Confirm vegetarian dining and indoor pool / spa access. Pre-arrange CDG transfer (~45 min).

5. **Book TGV INOUI Paris → Nice for 2 Jun.** 1st class, upper deck, table-of-4 (carré), right side facing forward. SNCF Connect app or sncf-connect.com. Bookings open 3-4 months ahead.

6. **Book TGV Nice ↔ Avignon round-trip.** Outbound: Fri 5 Jun ~09:00 Nice-Ville → Avignon TGV (direct, 2h56m). Return: Sun 7 Jun ~16:30 Avignon TGV → Nice-Ville (direct, ~3h). 1st class both ways. Total ~€500–700.

7. **Book Provence private driver for Sat 6 Jun.** Saint-Rémy + Carrières de Lumières + Les Baux viewpoint full-day driver (~6 hours). Auberge concierge can recommend. ~€350–450. Confirm vegetarian-friendly lunch venue, comfortable AC vehicle.

8. **Book Châteauneuf-du-Pape vineyard visit for Sun 7 Jun morning.** Email Château La Nerthe (or Domaine du Vieux Télégraphe) **3–4 weeks in advance** requesting a 10:00am English-language guided cellar tour and tasting for 2 adults + 1 junior (14). Auberge concierge can coordinate driver.

9. **Travel insurance with pre-existing condition cover — 1 week.** Tata AIG / ICICI Lombard / Bajaj Allianz "Travel Prime" or AXA Schengen. Conditions explicitly listed. Physician's note in English.

10. **VFS Mumbai Schengen appointment — 2 weeks.** Documentation pack: cover letter listing all destinations (Paris + Versailles + Nice + Avignon + Monaco), ITRs, bank statements, employment letter, school letter, all confirmations.

11. **Confirm dietary needs with all three hotels.** Email Trianon Palace, Hyatt Nice, Auberge de Cassagne 2 weeks pre-arrival. Vegetarian (no meat / fish / seafood; eggs and dairy fine), low-FODMAP-aware preferences.

12. **Pre-book Antibes restaurant for Thu 4 Jun.** Marché Provençal area or seafront with vegetarian options. ~1 month before.
